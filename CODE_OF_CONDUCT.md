# Code of conduct

We want FastRecorder to be a welcoming project for people of all experience levels and backgrounds.

Be respectful, patient and constructive. Critique code and ideas rather than people. Help newcomers ask questions, respect privacy and give credit for contributions.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, unwanted sexual attention, publishing another person's private information and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable in issues, discussions, pull requests or other project spaces.

The maintainer may edit/remove content, close a discussion, restrict participation or ban an account when necessary to protect the community. Decisions should consider context and be proportionate; repeated or severe misconduct may lead to immediate exclusion.

Report ordinary moderation concerns to the maintainer, [@neerajsunil](https://github.com/neerajsunil), using an appropriate project discussion. Do not post private or sensitive details publicly; request a private contact first. Security issues follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
